## 4 · The comparison varies four things at once ★核心

> This is the core of the motivation.
>
> The paper's headline is that different architectures leak through different vectors, so you need multi-vector auditing. To get there it compares four models.
>
> But look at what else changes. DNABERT-2 isn't just a different architecture — it's the only one with subword tokenization, it's the only masked model, and it's nine times bigger than SimpleDNALM. Evo is five hundred times bigger than everything else.
>
> When four variables move together you cannot attribute the effect to any one of them. The comparison is not identified.
>
> And I want to be clear that this isn't me second-guessing the work from outside. It's what the paper says about itself, on the next slide.

**中文：** 这是整个动机的核心。

论文的主要结论是不同架构通过不同向量泄漏，所以需要多向量审计。为了得到这个结论，它比较了四个模型。

但看看还有什么在变。DNABERT-2 不只是架构不同——它是唯一用子词分词的，唯一用掩码目标的，而且比 SimpleDNALM 大九倍。Evo 比其他所有模型大五百倍。

当四个变量一起动的时候，你没法把效应归给其中任何一个。这个比较是"未被识别"的。

我想说清楚，这不是我从外面事后质疑这项工作。这是论文自己说的，下一张。

> **停顿一下，让大家看表。这张的视觉冲击力最强。**

## 5 · The paper's own Discussion ★转折点

> This is the paper's own Discussion section.
>
> It says the methodology does not isolate the objective from confounding variables — and it names them: parameter count and tokenization strategy. And it says a controlled ablation on a fixed architecture would be needed to establish a causal relationship.
>
> So the experiment I want to run is the one the paper asks for. Alex's extension note asks for it too, in almost the same words.
>
> Now, here's the part I find genuinely interesting. That sentence hands me tokenization for free. It's not something I invented to make the project sound novel — it's the second variable the authors themselves flag as uncontrolled. And in DNA, unlike in text, you can actually vary it cleanly. That's the next slide.

**中文：** 这是论文自己的讨论部分。

它说方法没能把目标和混淆变量分开——而且点了名：参数量和分词策略。它还说要建立因果关系，需要在固定架构上做一个受控消融。

所以我想做的实验，正是论文要求的那个。Alex 的扩展笔记也在要求同一件事，用词几乎一样。

有意思的地方在这里。这句话等于白送了我"分词"这根轴。它不是我为了让课题显得新颖而发明的——它是作者自己标出来的、第二个没被控制的变量。而在 DNA 上，不像在文字上，你真的可以把它干净地变化。下一张讲这个。

> **这是整场最重要的一张。语气要平稳，不要有"我抓到你了"的意思。重点是"论文自己要求的实验"。**

## 6 · Why this experiment needs DNA

> Here's why this is a DNA paper and not a text paper.
>
> The same string of nucleotides, carrying exactly the same information, can be cut four different ways. One letter per token, three, six, or learned subwords. The content is identical. Only the granularity changes.
>
> You cannot do this in English. If you change an English tokenizer, you change what a token means semantically, and the vocabulary co-adapts to the corpus. Character-level English isn't a clean lossless representation the way A-C-G-T is.
>
> So this is an axis that exists in our domain and nowhere else. And note — the NIH proposal's own rationale asserts that the fixed four-letter alphabet may alter memorization dynamics in ways domain-agnostic evaluations miss. Nobody has tested that assertion. This makes it falsifiable.

**中文：** 这就是为什么这是一篇 DNA 论文而不是文字论文。

同一串核苷酸，携带完全相同的信息，可以有四种切法：一个字母一块、三个、六个，或者学出来的子词。内容一模一样，只有粒度变了。

在英文里做不到。改英文的分词器就改变了 token 的语义，而且词表会和语料共适应。字符级英文不是 A-C-G-T 那样干净的无损表示。

所以这是一根只存在于我们这个领域的轴。另外注意——NIH proposal 自己的 rationale 里说，固定的四字母表可能以领域无关评估捕捉不到的方式改变记忆动态。没人验证过这个断言。这个实验让它变得可证伪。

## 7 · The same axis, in different units

> One consequence of the tokenization difference is worth spelling out, because it's independent of everything else.
>
> Perplexity is computed per token. A ninety-six nucleotide probe is ninety-six tokens under a character tokenizer, thirty-two under a three-mer, sixteen under a six-mer. And the chance floor scales with it — four, sixty-four, four thousand.
>
> For DNABERT-2 we can't even compute the conversion, because its average token length isn't published anywhere, and it varies by corpus.
>
> Table 4 puts all four models on one axis. So part of the reported cross-model spread may be a units artifact rather than a memorization difference. Quantifying that is a direct contribution of my tokenization axis.
>
> In my design every metric is reported per nucleotide, so this can't happen.

**中文：** 分词差异有一个后果值得单独说，因为它独立于其他所有问题。

困惑度是按 token 算的。一个 96 核苷酸的探针，在字符分词下是 96 个 token，三联体下是 32 个，六联体下是 16 个。随机基线也跟着变——4、64、4096。

对 DNABERT-2 我们连换算都做不了，因为它的平均 token 长度任何地方都没公布，而且随语料变化。

Table 4 把四个模型放在同一根轴上。所以报告出来的跨模型差异里，可能有一部分是单位造成的假象，而不是记忆差异。量化这一点正是我这根分词轴的直接贡献。

我的设计里所有指标都按核苷酸报告，所以不会出现这个问题。

## 8 · The design

> Here's the design.
>
> One backbone — four layers, 512 hidden, about thirteen million parameters. Architecture fixed. Non-embedding parameter count fixed. Same data, same probe set, same optimization budget.
>
> Two axes. Four tokenizer granularities, four objectives from causal through masking at fifty percent. That's sixteen cells, about ninety runs with seeds and controls.
>
> The repetition tiers ride along inside each run at no extra cost — so I get the per-tier disaggregation the published Methods announced but never showed.
>
> One design detail worth flagging: the probes are embedded inside host windows and tagged when the data is built, not appended as separate short sequences. That means everything is length-matched by construction, so nothing can separate members from non-members on length alone.
>
> And the deliverable is a variance decomposition.

**中文：** 这是设计。

一个骨架——四层，隐藏维度 512，约一千三百万参数。架构固定，非嵌入参数量固定，同样的数据、同样的探针集、同样的优化预算。

两根轴。四种分词粒度，四种目标，从因果到 50% 掩码。十六个格子，加上种子和对照大约 90 次训练。

重复次数档位在每次训练内部搭便车，不增加成本——所以我能得到已发表方法部分宣布过但从未展示的分档结果。

有一个设计细节值得提：探针是**嵌在宿主序列内部**、在构造数据时就打好标签的，不是作为独立的短序列追加进去。这意味着所有序列长度天然一致，没有任何东西能仅凭长度区分成员和非成员。

交付物是一个方差分解。

## 9 · Why the result is guaranteed ★最想要反馈的一张

> This is the slide I most want reactions to.
>
> Most designs I considered have a binary headline. Does the ranking reverse? Does the effect exist? If the answer is no, the paper dissolves, and you find out in month four.
>
> A variance decomposition gives you a number regardless. If the tokenizer and the objective reproduce most of the published cross-model spread, the headline is that architecture was never identified, and these are first-order privacy design choices. If they account for half, the headline is the quantification itself. And if they account for almost nothing, then architecture genuinely does dominate under control — which validates Alex's result with a stronger experiment behind it, and that's a perfectly good paper too.
>
> For a first paper on a six-month clock, I weight that above a higher ceiling with a coin-flip outcome.

**中文：** 这张是我最想听意见的。

我考虑过的大多数设计，结论都是二元的：排序会不会反转？效应存不存在？如果答案是"不"，论文就散了，而且要到第四个月才发现。

方差分解无论如何都给你一个数。如果分词和目标复现了大部分已发表的跨模型差异，标题就是"架构从未被识别出来"，而这两个是一阶的隐私设计选择。如果解释了一半，标题就是这个量化本身。如果几乎什么都没解释，那说明在受控条件下架构确实主导——这就用更强的实验验证了 Alex 的结论，同样是一篇很好的论文。

对一个只有六个月的第一篇论文，我认为这比"天花板更高但结论是抛硬币"更值得。

## 10 · The 4.0 floor

> One thing from the foundations audit that I think is genuinely useful to us as a group.
>
> Uniform synthetic DNA has entropy of exactly two bits per nucleotide. That means held-out perplexity has a hard floor of four. It's a two-line theorem — cross entropy equals entropy plus KL divergence, and KL is non-negative. No model beats it at any scale.
>
> Natural language doesn't have this. There's no analytically known "no memorization" value in text. We have one. So it's a free correctness test on any scoring implementation before we trust it.
>
> The scope matters: it holds when scores are normalized per position and no position sees its own target. It does not transfer to real genomic sequence, or to subword-level masked scoring.
>
> And for calibration on why this matters — the entire usable range on a bacterial genome is about four down to three-seven. A stride change alone once moved GPT-2's reported perplexity by fifteen percent. An evaluation artifact can be larger than the whole effect you're trying to measure.
>
> I want to make this a build-breaking assertion in the code.

**中文：** 基础审计里有一条我觉得对我们整个组都有用。

均匀随机的合成 DNA，熵正好是每核苷酸 2 比特。这意味着留出集的困惑度有一个硬下限 4.0。这是两行就能证的定理——交叉熵等于熵加 KL 散度，而 KL 非负。任何规模的模型都突破不了。

自然语言没有这个。文字里没有一个解析已知的"零记忆"数值。我们有。所以这是一个免费的正确性检验，可以在信任任何打分实现之前先跑一遍。

适用范围很重要：它成立的条件是逐位置归一化，且没有任何位置能看到自己的目标。它不适用于真实基因组序列，也不适用于子词级的掩码打分。

说个数让大家体会为什么这重要——细菌基因组上全部可用区间大约是 4.00 到 3.70。而光是改变评估时的 stride，就曾让 GPT-2 报告的困惑度变动 15%。评估上的一个假象，可以比你想测的整个效应还大。

我想把这个做成代码里的强制断言。

## 11 · Six experiments

> Six experiments, four core.
>
> E0 calibrates the instruments — every metric run against a corpus where the answer is known in closed form, plus a blind baseline that uses only surface statistics of the probe with no model access. If the blind baseline matches the model-based attack, we're measuring distribution shift, not memorization.
>
> E1 is the grid, and the headline.
>
> E2 varies the mask rate continuously and plots causal at the far end, to test whether masked-versus-causal is qualitative or just a rescaling.
>
> E3 is the one I think is methodologically most interesting. To compare a causal and a masked model fairly you have to equalize how much the adversary reveals. On uniform DNA that's exact — K revealed nucleotides is exactly 2K bits. Existing work matches on token counts, which breaks the moment tokenizers differ. And as far as the audit could establish, there's no formal treatment of matched conditioning for this comparison anywhere. That's a real gap.
>
> E4 and E5 are stretch. E5 is Alex's corpus-scaling extension, carried as a control.

**中文：** 六个实验，四个是核心。

E0 校准仪器——每个指标都在一个答案有闭式解的语料上跑一遍，再加一个"盲基线"：完全不看模型、只用探针的表面统计特征。如果盲基线和基于模型的攻击打平，说明我们测的是分布偏移，不是记忆。

E1 是网格，也是主线。

E2 连续改变掩码比例，把因果模型画在最远端，检验掩码和因果的区别是本质的还是只是程度差异。

E3 是我认为方法学上最有意思的。要公平比较因果模型和掩码模型，必须让攻击者透露的信息量相等。在均匀 DNA 上这是精确的——透露 K 个核苷酸正好是 2K 比特。现有工作按 token 数量对齐，一旦分词器不同就失效。而据审计所能查证，这个比较的"对齐条件"在文献里没有任何形式化处理。这是一个真实的空白。

E4 和 E5 是选做。E5 是 Alex 的语料缩放扩展，作为对照带进来。

## 12 · What it costs

> Cost.
>
> Under eight gigabytes of disk for the entire project. I'm leading with that number because storage is the constraint Alex named, and this design was chosen partly to avoid it.
>
> Compute is four 4090s that I own, so the core has no HPC dependency and no queue. Zero shadow models. Zero human subject data, so no IRB and no dbGaP application.
>
> For comparison: a proper reference-model LiRA evaluation needs on the order of seven hundred training runs. A corpus-scaling grid including Evo could reach the terabyte range.
>
> On Evo — I'm excluding it, and not only for cost. In the published setup a fresh full-rank output head is trained alongside the low-rank adapter, so "LoRA-only" doesn't quite describe the optimization. Disentangling adapter capacity from readout capacity is its own project. I'd rather scope it out and say so in Limitations.
>
> I'd still like HPC access as a backstop and for the external-validity arm.

**中文：** 成本。

整个项目磁盘占用不到八 GB。我先说这个数，因为存储是 Alex 提过的唯一约束，而这个设计部分就是为了避开它。

算力是我自己的四张 4090，所以核心实验不依赖 HPC，也不用排队。零影子模型。零人类受试者数据，所以不需要 IRB，不需要 dbGaP 申请。

作个对比：一次正规的参考模型 LiRA 评估需要七百多次训练。包含 Evo 的语料缩放网格可能到 TB 级。

关于 Evo——我要排除它，不只是因为成本。在已发表的设置里，一个全新的全秩输出头是和低秩适配器一起训练的，所以"只用 LoRA"不太准确地描述了这个优化过程。把适配器容量和读出层容量分开是另一个课题。我宁愿划到范围外，在 Limitations 里说明。

我仍然希望有 HPC 作为后备，以及用于外部有效性那一支。

> **"LoRA-only doesn't quite describe the optimization" 这句要说得中性、轻描淡写。这是关于实验范围的陈述，不是指控。**

## 14 · What this leaves open

> Two things I'm deliberately not consuming, and four things I need.
>
> Aim 1.2, the defenses, stays open and I'd like it as paper two. One caveat I want on the record now: at a thirteen-million-parameter from-scratch scale, the downstream utility axis may not move measurably, which would make a privacy-utility tradeoff plot uninformative. Establishing a usable utility protocol is a prerequisite worth scoping before anyone commits.
>
> The second is kinship leakage. The proposal motivates GLM privacy partly on heritability — that a memorized sequence exposes relatives who never consented — and nothing in the published work measures it. 1000 Genomes gives us 602 complete trios, fully open, no application. I think that's the highest-ceiling direction we have and the least likely to be scooped by an NLP group. It's also data-engineering-first in the area where I'm weakest, so I'd rather it be paper three than paper one. I'd like to stake it out.
>
> What I need: a decision on venue, the CCS 2015 paper from Erman since he's an author and neither library will serve it, a route to an HPC allocation, and your view on authorship.
>
> Next week I measure real throughput on my own cards, confirm the deadlines, and sit down with Alex on implementation.
>
> Thank you — happy to take questions.

## 预判问题与回答

**Q: Why not a bioinformatics venue?（为什么不投生物信息学的会？）**

> The claim shape is a mechanism paper — a variance decomposition and a dose-response curve — which is what ML main tracks reward. Security venues reward audits, which this isn't. But the same experiment set supports an ISMB framing with the emphasis shifted to practical guidance for GLM practitioners on tokenizer and objective selection. I'd take your view on it.

（这个结论的形态是机制论文——方差分解加剂量反应曲线——这正是 ML 主会奖励的。安全类会议奖励的是审计型工作，这篇不是。但同一套实验换个侧重就能做成 ISMB 的框架，重点转向给 GLM 使用者的分词器和目标选择建议。我听你们的。）

**Q: Isn't this just re-running Alex's experiments?（这不就是重跑 Alex 的实验吗？）**

> No — different models entirely. Alex compared four existing pretrained models. I train about ninety from scratch on one fixed backbone. Not one core experiment compares against a published number. The published work motivates it; it doesn't supply the data.

（不是——完全不同的模型。Alex 比较的是四个现成的预训练模型。我在一个固定骨架上从零训练约九十个。没有任何一个核心实验去和已发表的数字做对比。已发表的工作提供动机，不提供数据。）

**Q: Thirteen million parameters is tiny. Will this generalize?（一千三百万参数太小了，能推广吗？）**

> That's the honest limitation and it goes in the paper. It buys me the control — I can't fix architecture and vary only tokenization on models someone else trained. E4 is the external-validity arm that tests whether the fit predicts DNABERT-2 and HyenaDNA. If it doesn't, that's a finding too.

（这是诚实的局限，会写进论文。它换来的是控制力——在别人训好的模型上，我没法固定架构只改分词。E4 就是外部有效性那一支，检验这个拟合能不能预测 DNABERT-2 和 HyenaDNA。如果不能，那也是一个发现。）

**Q: What if the tokenizer effect is zero?（如果分词效应是零呢？）**

> Then architecture genuinely dominates under control, and we've validated the published result with a stronger experiment. That's branch three on slide nine. It's a real outcome, not a failure.

（那说明在受控条件下架构确实主导，我们就用更强的实验验证了已发表的结论。这是第九张的第三个分支。这是一个真实的结果，不是失败。）
